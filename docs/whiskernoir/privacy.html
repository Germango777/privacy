Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Whisker Noir - Detective app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by Dagermavid (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application collects no personal information when you download and use it. Registration is not required and no account is created. The Application does not access your contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal data.

The only data stored on your device is your case progress, star ratings and sound preference. This data is saved inside the Application's own local storage, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the Application.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Application uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob, as a third party, may collect and process data including:

  • Device advertising ID (for serving relevant ads)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General location (non-precise, for geo-targeted ads)
  • Usage data (app interactions, session duration)
  • Advertising interactions (ad impressions and clicks)

These data are handled under Google's own privacy terms. You can review how Google uses data at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and personalised advertising

Where required by law, the Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before showing personalised advertising. If a consent form is shown, you can accept, decline, or manage your choice. While a choice is pending, the Application does not request ads at all.

If a button labelled "Ad privacy" appears next to the advertisement, you can reopen the consent form at any time and change your choice.

You can also opt out of personalised advertising through your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop all data collection by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the app marketplace.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding your information. Since the Application itself collects no personal information, there is no risk of your data being accessed by unauthorized individuals. Data handled by third parties such as Google is protected under their respective policies.
