Privacy Policy

Wasteland: Zombie Survival

Effective date and last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices of Dagermavid ("the developer", "we", "us") for the Android game Wasteland: Zombie Survival ("the Game"), package name com.wasteland.app.

1. Information collected by the Game

The Game does not require a personal account or registration. This policy covers the Google AdMob test-banner integration being implemented using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy choices. It does not claim that production advertising or consent configuration has been validated.

The developer does not operate a player-data collection service. Local game settings and session data are described in Section 2; Google's SDK processing is described separately in Section 3. The Game does not ask you to provide or grant access to:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or account credentials.
  • Precise GPS location or device location permissions.
  • Contacts, calendar entries, photos, videos or other personal files.
  • Camera or microphone recordings.

This is not a claim that no data leaves your device: advertising and consent SDKs may make network requests and process identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, diagnostics and other information even when only test advertisements are used.

Camera flashes shown by zombie characters are visual game effects; the Game does not use your device's camera.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your music, sound-effects and ambience volume preferences locally in its private app storage so that these settings can be restored when you reopen it. These preferences are used only to operate the Game and are not uploaded by the Game.

Wave progress, health, kills and weapon upgrades are used during the current play session. The current version does not upload these values or maintain an online player profile or leaderboard.

The current Android build disables automatic app-data backup. You can remove saved settings through your device's app settings by clearing the Game's storage, or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Advertising, analytics and third-party services

Google AdMob test banners. The integration uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google's official Android demo app ID ca-app-pub-3940256099942544~3347511713 and demo banner ad unit ID ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/6300978111, rather than production advertising IDs. Test ads are for integration testing; they do not make the SDK offline or data-free. Google and its service providers may process information through the advertising and consent SDKs. The information processed depends on SDK configuration, the advertisements served, your region, device settings and applicable consent choices.

This information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app-instance identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address, not GPS location requested by the Game.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, video views and other interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, including SDK crash and error data.

These data may be used to deliver and personalize advertisements where permitted, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, maintain security and improve advertising services. Non-personalized or limited ads may still involve processing certain information for ad delivery, measurement or fraud prevention; they do not necessarily mean that no data is collected.

Consent and privacy options. The integration uses Google UMP to update consent information and show a consent form when required. Mobile Ads initialization and banner loading are gated on UMP indicating that ads may be requested; they do not proceed while that gate is closed. UMP may itself contact Google to determine consent requirements and process consent choices. A native Ad privacy button is provided when UMP reports that a privacy-options entry point is required, allowing you to reopen the applicable form and review or change your choices.

Your choices may affect which ads can be shown. Android's privacy and advertising settings may also allow you to reset or delete your advertising ID or manage ad personalization; available options vary by Android version. Changing these settings does not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing.

Google and its partners may process advertising information on servers outside your country. Their handling, retention and deletion of that information are described in their applicable policies. For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy.
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google AdMob privacy and advertising information.

The Game does not include a separate analytics SDK, social sign-in, in-app purchases or an integrated cloud-save service. Advertising and consent SDK processing described above is separate from locally stored game preferences and may include diagnostics and ad measurement.

The Game is built with the Godot Engine. No developer-operated server is needed to play, and the developer does not receive local game settings or session statistics through a separate telemetry service.

Google Play and Android may independently process information related to downloading, installing, updating or diagnosing apps according to their own settings and policies. This platform processing is separate from the Game's own functionality. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Device permissions

The test advertising integration declares these Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: network access for advertising and consent requests.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: checking network connectivity.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: allowing the advertising SDK to access the Android advertising ID where available and permitted by device settings and applicable choices.

Declaring these permissions does not mean that ads can bypass the UMP gate described in Section 3. The Game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, personal media or shared-storage permissions. Access to its own private storage is used for local settings.

5. Contacting the developer

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we receive the email address you use, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request, provide support and resolve reported issues. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Support correspondence may be processed by the email service used to exchange messages. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations.

6. Children

The developer does not ask children or other players to submit personal information in order to play. Advertising and consent SDK processing is described in Section 3. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

This policy does not establish an age restriction or certify that production audience, consent or child-directed advertising settings have been validated. Advertising configuration and any required age-related safeguards must reflect the Game's declared audience and applicable children's privacy requirements.

7. Security and your choices

Local settings are stored using Android's standard app-private storage protections. We do not maintain a server-side player database. Nevertheless, no device or method of electronic communication can be guaranteed completely secure.

You can play without creating an account, change your sound preferences, clear local app storage or uninstall the Game at any time. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete personal information you have voluntarily provided to us, or to restrict or object to its processing. Contact us to make a request. There is no in-game account to delete in the current version.

Advertising-related privacy choices, including the UMP privacy-options button when required, are described in Section 3. Clearing local storage or uninstalling the Game does not by itself delete information already processed by Google or advertising partners. Consult their policies and privacy tools for requests concerning that information.

If production advertising or additional data-processing features are introduced, the policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated, and any required notices or consent requests will be provided.
